Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before you invest.

Have a specific technical question? Speak directly with our engineering desk at +91 81481 33644.

How much solar subsidy can I get in Tamil Nadu and Kerala?

Eligible residential households in Tamil Nadu can receive up to ₹1,00,000 in combined government subsidies—comprising up to ₹78,000 from the Central Government (PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana) and up to ₹22,000 from the Tamil Nadu State additional rooftop solar subsidy (applicable to eligible registrations made on or after 5 August 2026, subject to scheme guidelines and commissioning verification). Eligible households in Kerala qualify for up to ₹78,000 under the Central PM Surya Ghar scheme. Subsidies are credited directly to your bank account via DBT after commissioning and inspection.

Can solar panels reduce my electricity bill to zero?

A properly sized on-grid solar system can offset up to 90–100% of your variable electricity consumption charges via TANGEDCO or KSEB bi-directional net-metering. Statutory fixed charges, meter rent, or minimum utility fees still apply depending on your tariff slab, so actual savings depend on your roof area, sunlight, and usage pattern.

What is the difference between On-Grid, Off-Grid, and Hybrid systems?

On-Grid systems connect directly to the utility grid for net-metering, deliver the fastest payback, and qualify for government subsidies. Off-Grid systems rely on batteries for locations without dependable grid supply. Hybrid (BESS) systems combine grid bill savings with automatic battery backup during power outages.

Do you execute commercial and industrial solar plants—and what is the ROI?

Yes. We design and commission rooftop and ground-mounted EPC plants for factories, textile mills, hospitals, colleges, and commercial buildings. Businesses benefit from high-tension (HT) tariff offset and up to 40% Accelerated Tax Depreciation under the Income Tax Act, typically achieving capital payback in 2.5 to 3.5 years depending on tariff structure and load profile.

What warranties and after-sales maintenance are included?

Tier-1 solar modules carry up to a 25-year linear performance warranty, while inverters, mounting structures, and workmanship warranties are specified transparently in your system quotation. We provide real-time app monitoring setup, scheduled inspection options, and responsive regional service support from our local engineering hubs.

Which locations does Suntastic Engineering serve?

We execute residential, commercial, industrial, and government solar projects across Tamil Nadu and Kerala, supported by our Registered Office in Chennai (Chrompet), our Coimbatore Hub (Madukkarai), and regional branches in Palakkad and Thiruvananthapuram.
