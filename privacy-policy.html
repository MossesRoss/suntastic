Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Who we are

Suntastic Engineering Private Limited (“Suntastic”, “we”, “us”) operates suntasticsolarsystems.com.

  • Registered office: No. 69/27A, First Pillaiyar Koil Street, Lakshmipuram, Chrompet, Chennai – 600 044

2. Information we collect

  • Enquiry details you submit through our forms: property type, name, mobile number, email (optional), average monthly electricity bill and pincode.
  • Details you share by phone, WhatsApp, email or during site visits.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends to our web host, such as IP address, device and pages viewed.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals and plan site surveys.
  • To assist with subsidy and net-metering applications (for example PM Surya Ghar) when you ask us to.
  • For installation, warranty, maintenance and after-sales support.
  • To improve our website and services, and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

4. Consent

We process your personal data with your consent, which you give when you submit a form or contact us, or for other uses permitted under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as web hosting, Google Workspace and Google Sheets (where enquiry records are kept) and WhatsApp for messaging.
  • Government portals, DISCOMs and agencies, when needed for your application and with your knowledge.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

6. WhatsApp and third-party services

Our enquiry form opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled; messages you send there are governed by WhatsApp’s own policies. Our pages also load fonts and icons from Google Fonts and cdnjs, so those providers may receive your IP address. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics tools, we will update this policy.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and project records for as long as needed for your enquiry, installation, warranty and service, and for legal and accounting requirements. After that we delete or anonymise them.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

You may ask us to give you a summary of the personal data we hold about you, correct or update it, erase it, or withdraw your consent. You may also nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf. Write to us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children

Our website is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
